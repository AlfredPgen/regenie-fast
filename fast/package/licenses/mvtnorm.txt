MVTDST: Fortran code by Alan Genz for multivariate normal and t probabilities, included in regenie as
external_libs/mvtnorm (mvt.f, randomF77.c, plus regenie's C wrapper mvtnorm.cpp) and compiled into both programs.
==============================================================================

mvt.f states its origin as follows:

    $Id: mvt.f 231 2011-11-07 13:48:07Z thothorn $
    A subroutine for computing non-central multivariate t probabilities.
    This subroutine uses an algorithm (QRSVN) described in the paper
    "Comparison of Methods for the Computation of Multivariate
        t-Probabilities", by Alan Genz and Frank Bretz
        J. Comp. Graph. Stat. 11 (2002), pp. 950-971.
         Alan Genz, Department of Mathematics, Washington State University
    Original source available from
    http://www.math.wsu.edu/faculty/genz/software/fort77/mvtdstpack.f

The "$Id" line identifies the copy as taken from the R package mvtnorm
(https://cran.r-project.org/package=mvtnorm), which is distributed under GPL-2. randomF77.c also comes
from that package; in regenie it contains only three one-line stub functions. Neither file states a
licence of its own, and regenie's LICENSE does not mention them. Alan Genz has also released his
multivariate normal code under BSD-style terms elsewhere (for example the copy in SciPy).
