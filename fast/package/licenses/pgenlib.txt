pgenlib: the PLINK 2.0 .pgen reader (pgenlibr), included in regenie as external_libs/pgenlib and compiled
into both programs (static library pgenlib.a). Upstream: https://github.com/chrchang/plink-ng (2.0/pgenlibr).
regenie's copy was modified by Joelle Mbatchou (2020) to remove the R interface.
